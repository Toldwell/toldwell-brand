Style

Strive for true-to-life photography in all our visuals. Remember the following style principles:

Atmosphere

With carefully controlled lighting and shadow play, these images convey depth and emotion, adding an element of drama to any narrative.

Moments

Capture snapshots of real-life work experiences, showcasing the essence of Toldwell's context and its impact across industries.

Candidness

Opt for candid photography over staged shots to convey genuine moments and minimize a forced feel.

People

Our workplace imagery showcases people in action within Toldwell's vibrant environment, whether in dimly lit offices or bustling workspaces. Devices play a central role, symbolizing the seamless integration of technology into our workflow.

Devices

Device-focused imagery highlights the seamless integration of technology into Toldwell's workflow. Devices play a central role, symbolizing productivity and innovation.

Photography

Behind-the-scenes from real Toldwell sets. Cinematic, candid, never staged corporate stock. The work is the brand.

Style principles

  • Real sets, real crews, real moments — never composite or staged stock
  • Natural light when possible; cinematic side-light, godrays, and haze when working
  • Black-and-white treatment is welcome for editorial weight; saturation should never feel artificial
  • Subjects in motion or in thought, never posed for the camera
  • Equipment-forward shots are encouraged — the craft is part of the brand
